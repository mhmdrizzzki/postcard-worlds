How it works

  • Your scene is view 1, drawn with a fixed seed so the style stays steady.
  • Every view offers three ways on: a door, a path and a window.
  • The next postcard is drawn from the route you took, so the world keeps some coherence.
  • The breadcrumb trail and the replay link let you — or a friend — walk the same route again.
